# Travel-wallet benefits

- Read `docs/travel-wallet-benefits.md` before editing `database/data/held-card-benefits.json` or other travel-wallet catalog JSON.
- `description` is human notes only. Do not invent ranking rules from it.
- Encode restrictions in columns: `applies_to`, `required_channel`, `allowed_vendors`, `award_only`, `quantity_total`, `max_apply_per_use`, location, `program`.
